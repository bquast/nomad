NOMAD

Walk procedural planets. Mine resources, scan the wildlife, refuel your ship, fly to space without a loading screen, then warp to a new star system.

Desktop on footWASD move, mouse look, Space jetpack, Shift sprint, click mine, F scan, E board ship, Tab menu
Desktop in shipW throttle, S brake, mouse pitch/yaw, A/D roll, Shift boost, Space pulse drive (in space), click laser, E land / exit, V cockpit or chase view
Quest on footLeft stick move (click = sprint), right stick turn, A jetpack (swim up in water), right trigger mine, X scan, B board, Y menu, grip on panel = move panel
Quest in shipLeft trigger or left stick up = throttle, left stick X yaw, right stick pitch/roll, hold right grip = hand stick, A boost, X pulse drive, right trigger laser, B land / exit
GamepadLeft stick move, right stick look, RT mine, LT throttle, A jetpack/boost, X scan, B interact, Start menu
